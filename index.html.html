About the Conference

Advancing Intelligent Energy Systems, Electronics and Control

The International Conference on Intelligent Energy Systems, Electronics and Control – 2027 (ICIESEC–2027) is organized by the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Thiagarajar College of Engineering, Madurai, on 9th and 10th December 2027.

ICIESEC–2027 is an international forum for exploring recent advancements and addressing emerging research challenges in intelligent energy systems, electronics and control. The conference brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, research scholars and students to exchange knowledge and present innovative solutions for sustainable engineering.

The conference focuses on emerging developments in green energy technologies, smart electronics, intelligent control systems, artificial intelligence, power electronics, electric vehicles, smart grids and sustainable engineering.

About the Institution

Thiagarajar College of Engineering, Madurai

Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) was established in 1957 by the philanthropist Late Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar. It is a Government Aided Autonomous Institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

TCE offers Undergraduate, Postgraduate and PhD programmes across Engineering, Architecture and Science. The campus sits in a green, eco-friendly setting at the foot of Thiruparankundram Hill, with modern infrastructure for teaching and research.

The institution follows a Competency-Based Curriculum and Outcome-Based Education, and since 2018 has used the Conceive–Design–Implement–Operate (CDIO) framework to shape curriculum design, teaching and assessment, with strong hands-on training.

TCE launched its own MOOCs in 2021 and runs the Thiagarajar Research Fellowship (TRF) scheme for PhD scholars. In 2024 the campus received a Platinum Certificate in the 5S Audit for cleanliness and sustainability.

About the Department

Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) was established in 1957, the year the college was founded. Its UG and PG programmes are accredited, and the department has followed Outcome-Based Education since 2014–15.

The department is supported by the DST–FIST programme and received the AICTE–CII Best Industry-Linked Institute Award in 2014. It works closely with industry partners including TVS Motors, General Electric, Honeywell, CPRI and CDAC, and runs the TCE–TVSM Collaborated Education Program under an MoU with TVS.

Faculty members have received the AICTE Career Award for Young Teachers, the TNSCST Young Scientist Fellowship, post-doctoral research fellowships and national-level educator awards.

Paper Submission

Author Guidelines

Read the guidelines carefully before you submit through the conference portal.

  1. ICIESEC–2027 invites original, full-length research papers and review articles.
  2. The manuscript should be 6–8 pages in the prescribed IEEE Conference Paper Format.
  3. Explain the contribution, methodology or analysis, findings and conclusions clearly.
  4. Include all authors' names, affiliations and email addresses, and identify the corresponding author.
  5. Submit work that has not been published and is not under review elsewhere.
  6. Submit through the conference's online paper submission portal.
  7. All submissions undergo peer review. The decision and any requested revisions are sent to the corresponding author.
  8. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present it at the conference.
  9. Submit the final camera-ready manuscript and the copyright form by the announced deadline.
